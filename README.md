# powerBI-ai-workforce-automation-dashboard
Power BI dashboard analyzing AI investment, automation, workforce impact, productivity, and AI readiness from 2015 to 2025.

Project Name: AI Workforce & Automation Impact Dashboard

## Overview

This project is a three-page interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes AI investment, automation adoption, workforce impact, productivity, and AI readiness across 20 countries from 2015 to 2025.

The objective of this project is to transform a public dataset into an interactive business intelligence dashboard and explore how AI adoption is associated with employment outcomes, job displacement, productivity, and national AI readiness.

This project was developed as part of my Data Analyst portfolio to demonstrate dashboard design, business analysis, and data storytelling skills using Power BI.

---

# Dataset

**Source:** Kaggle

**Dataset:** AI Workforce & Automation Dataset (2015–2025)

**File Used:**
`global_ai_workforce_automation_2015_2025.csv`

The dataset contains country-level indicators including:

- Year
- Country
- AI Investment (Billion USD)
- Automation Rate (%)
- Employment Rate (%)
- Average Salary (USD)
- Productivity Index
- Reskilling Investment (Million USD)
- AI Policy Index
- Job Creation (Million)
- Job Displacement (Million)
- AI Readiness Score

**Link:** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/emirhanakku/ai-workforce-and-automation-dataset-20152025 

> **Note:** This project uses a publicly available Kaggle dataset for portfolio and analytical purposes. The findings describe patterns observed in the dataset and should not be interpreted as causal conclusions.

---

# Business Questions

This dashboard was designed to answer the following questions:

- How have AI investment and automation adoption changed from 2015 to 2025?
- Which countries have the highest average AI investment?
- Does automation show a clear relationship with employment outcomes?
- Is higher automation associated with greater job displacement?
- How do job creation and job displacement compare?
- Is AI investment associated with higher productivity?

# Key Findings

- AI investment and automation rate both increased steadily from 2015 to 2025.
- Average AI investment varies substantially across countries, with a small group of countries leading overall investment.
- Automation rate does not show a clear relationship with employment rate, but it is generally associated with higher job displacement.
- Job creation represents a larger share than job displacement in this dataset.
- Higher AI investment is generally associated with higher productivity, although the relationship is not perfectly linear.
